🔒 Privacy Policy

Focus Champion · Last updated: February 2026 (Bahman 1404)

✅ No personal data collected

📋 Overview

Focus Champion is a productivity timer app. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data is used and how.

💾 Data We Collect

We do NOT collect any personal data.

All app data is stored locally on your device only:

  • Session history and statistics
  • Level and streak progress
  • App preferences (language, sound)
  • Premium activation status

This data never leaves your device and is not sent to any server.

📡 Internet Access

The app requires internet access only for:

  • Loading alarm sounds (from public CDN)
  • Displaying advertisements (via Google AdMob)

📢 Advertising (Google AdMob)

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect:

  • Device advertising ID
  • IP address (approximate location)
  • Device information

Please review Google's Privacy Policy for details.

Opt out: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization

💳 Payments

Optional donations are processed via PayPal. We do not collect or store any payment information.

🔔 Notifications

The app sends local notifications when a focus session ends. These are generated on your device only — no server involved.

👶 Children's Privacy

Focus Champion does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
