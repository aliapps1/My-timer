Privacy Policy for Focus Timer Pro

Last Updated: February 6, 2026

1. Introduction

Focus Timer Pro ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles your information.

2. Information We Collect

Focus Timer Pro operates entirely on your device and does NOT collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers.

Local Storage Only:

  • Timer Statistics: Your daily focus time, weekly totals, and streak data are stored locally on your device using browser localStorage
  • User Preferences: Language selection and sound preferences are saved locally
  • Timer State: Active timer sessions are stored locally to persist across app restarts

3. How We Use Information

All data stored by Focus Timer Pro remains on your device and is used solely to:

  • Display your productivity statistics
  • Maintain your focus streak
  • Remember your preferences
  • Restore timer state after closing the app

4. Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or transmit any user data. All information stays on your device. When you use the "Share Result" feature, you manually control what information is shared through your device's native sharing functionality.

5. Data Storage and Security

  • All data is stored locally using browser localStorage API
  • No data is sent to external servers
  • No user accounts or authentication required
  • No analytics or tracking services are used

6. Permissions

Focus Timer Pro may request the following permissions:

  • Notifications: To alert you when a focus session is complete (optional, user-granted)
  • Audio: To play completion sounds (optional, can be muted)

7. Children's Privacy

Focus Timer Pro does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The app can be safely used by all ages.

8. Data Deletion

You can delete all your data at any time by:

  • Clearing your browser's localStorage for this app
  • Uninstalling the application
  • Clearing browser data/cache

9. Third-Party Services

Focus Timer Pro does not use any third-party services, analytics, or advertising networks.
